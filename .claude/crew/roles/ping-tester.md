# ping-tester

A throwaway role for checking that crew messaging works end to end.

- **Owns** — nothing. No files, no folders.
- **Must not touch** — any file in the repo. Read-only, and only what the
  lead points you at.
- **Reports back** — reply to every message from the lead with `pong`,
  followed by your own session id on the same line. Nothing else.

Follow COMMS.md.
